## The Hook

When a real crisis hits, massive movie hype takes a back seat. The team behind the upcoming epic 'Ramayana' called off their grand Navratri launch in Ramtek to prioritize farmer relief during an ongoing state drought.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- The major Ramtek promotional event featuring Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi was postponed due to severe drought in Maharashtra.
- Local MLA Ashish Jaiswal confirmed the delay, noting the production team offered to support struggling farmers.
- US theater listings reveal the project will feature a shorter English cut running two hours and 45 minutes alongside the full Hindi cut.

## The Breakdown

The makers of 'Ramayana Part 1' had a massive Navratri launch set for October 11, 2026, in Ramtek. Cast members including Ranbir Kapoor and Sai Pallavi were expected to debut a new track and meet fans at the historic location.

Local MLA Ashish Jaiswal announced the cancellation over Instagram, clarifying that farmers facing drought across Maharashtra need immediate attention and resources. The film's production team agreed to pause festivities and reportedly pledged aid to impacted agricultural communities.

Meanwhile, overseas theater listings revealed fresh details about the global rollout plan. US chain AMC posted a two-hour-and-45-minute runtime for an English version titled 'Ramayana: Rise of a Legend,' which runs 30 minutes shorter than the original Hindi cut.

## Why It Matters

Big-budget entertainment spectacles can easily look tone-deaf when local communities face real environmental and economic stress. Hitting pause to support local farmers keeps priorities grounded while the project gears up for a high-stakes worldwide release.
