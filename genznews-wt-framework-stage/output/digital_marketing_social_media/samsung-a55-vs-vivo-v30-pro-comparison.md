## The Breakdown

Samsung expanded its A-series lineup with the Galaxy A55, bringing an Exynos chip, a 50MP main camera, and solid build quality to the mid-tier market. Prices kick off at Rs 39,999, stretching up to Rs 45,999 if you want the maxed-out 12GB RAM version.

Meanwhile, Vivo dropped the V30 Pro at a similar Rs 41,999 starting price. It brings serious hardware upgrades to the table, including a curved AMOLED screen, a higher base storage tier of 256GB, and a versatile telephoto lens for better portraits.

Charging speeds are where things get brutally lopsided. Vivo supports blazing-fast 80W charging, while Samsung is still dragging its feet with a disappointing 25W limit.
