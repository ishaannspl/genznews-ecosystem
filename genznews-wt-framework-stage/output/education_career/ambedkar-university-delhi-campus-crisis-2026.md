## The Hook

When a public university starts treating doctoral researchers like desk-bound office staff with punch clocks, student blowback is inevitable. At Ambedkar University Delhi, that policy sparked an intense campus standoff that exposes much deeper institutional cracks.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- PhD researchers launched sit-ins after the administration mandated daily attendance logs and threatened stipend deductions for routine fieldwork.
- The university met peaceful demonstrations with locked campus gates, police barricades, and private security guards.
- Beyond surveillance, students face crumbling campus facilities, surging semester fees, and virtually non-existent student housing.

## The Breakdown

The unrest kicked off when the Dean of Academic Affairs mandated a daily whereabouts register for doctoral scholars, requiring prior bureaucratic permission just to visit archives or conduct fieldwork. Administrators reportedly dismissed student concerns, comparing research scholars to entry-level office staff and claiming their stipends function as wages that require strict time tracking.

When researchers staged an indefinite sit-in on September 28, the administration deployed private security and barricaded campus gates, blocking food and essential medicine from reaching protesters. This institutional crackdown follows a March 2026 Delhi High Court ruling that overturned the wrongful expulsion of a student protester, stating universities cannot punish students for peaceful dissent.

The conflict also highlights glaring inequalities on campus, where students pay significantly higher fees than peers at other public universities while navigating leaking classrooms, exposed wiring, and broken sanitation. To make matters worse, upfront fee waivers for marginalized students have been replaced by delayed reimbursement systems, and hostel access remains practically non-existent.

## Why It Matters

Public universities are supposed to provide accessible, critical education, but AUD shows what happens when public institutions adopt corporate management tactics. For students navigating higher education, this ongoing fight highlights the steep hidden costs, bureaucratic surveillance, and housing shortages increasingly threatening academic freedom and affordability.
