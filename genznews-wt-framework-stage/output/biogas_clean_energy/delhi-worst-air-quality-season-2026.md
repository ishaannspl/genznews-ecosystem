## The Breakdown

Delhi recorded a 24-hour average AQI of 187 on Sunday, landing on the higher end of the moderate category. At least 19 monitoring stations dipped into poor air quality territory as low wind speeds trapped local emissions.

Neighbouring cities fared even worse, with Noida hitting 281 and Ghaziabad touching 248. Authorities warn that dropping temperatures and incoming northwesterly winds will likely push air quality down further over the next ten days.

## Why It Matters

Severe urban air pollution is a massive public health crisis that demands aggressive clean energy transitions, better waste management, and green fuel adoption. When regional air turns toxic, daily routines shift indoors, highlighting why local governments must fund scalable biogas and clean energy infrastructure to cut baseline emissions.
