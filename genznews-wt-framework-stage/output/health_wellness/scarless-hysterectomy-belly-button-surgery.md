## The Hook

Imagine undergoing major reproductive surgery and walking away with zero visible marks across your stomach. London surgeons just pulled off Europe's first hysterectomy performed entirely through a patient's belly button.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- Surgeons in London completed a hysterectomy through a single tiny incision hidden directly inside the navel.
- The procedure, known as single-incision laparoscopic surgery, replaces the standard three to four cuts with just one.
- The patient chose the breakthrough operation after suffering for years from adenomyosis, a painful uterine condition.

## The Breakdown

Traditional keyhole surgery typically requires doctors to poke three or four separate holes across the abdomen to fit cameras and tools. In this operation, lead surgeon Thomas Ind and his team condensed every instrument into one central entry point tucked inside the belly button.

Surgeons navigated the camera and tools using a video monitor to complete the extraction without leaving outward stomach scars. The patient, Debbie Price, opted for the operation after dealing with adenomyosis, an agonizing issue where womb lining grows directly into the uterine muscle wall.

## Why It Matters

Minimally invasive surgical tech is not just about aesthetics: it means smaller physical trauma and better clinical options for chronic gynecological pain. When surgical methods evolve, patients dealing with severe reproductive disorders get life-changing relief without carrying visible scars.
