## The Hook

Flu season panic is real, but stocking up on heavy pharmaceuticals is not your only line of defense. Simple Ayurvedic staples and everyday habits can help strengthen your body before seasonal bugs strike.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- Herbal staples like Tulsi, Giloi, raw garlic, and warm turmeric milk offer natural daily immunity boosts.
- Basic habits such as Pranayam breathwork and rigorous 20-second handwashing remain essential physical barriers.
- These lifestyle steps act as preventive shields, though they do not substitute for medical treatment if you already have the flu.

## The Breakdown

Protecting your respiratory tract starts with accessible pantry remedies. Chewing fresh Tulsi leaves, drinking Giloi herbal tea, or sipping warm milk mixed with turmeric provides traditional support against seasonal bugs. Adding raw garlic in the morning and getting natural Vitamin C through Amla juice also supports overall immunity.

Beyond what you ingest, simple physical routines matter just as much. Practicing yogic breathwork like Pranayam alongside light morning cardio keeps your lungs clear and resilient. Combine that with disciplined handwashing using warm water and soap for at least 20 seconds to cut infection risks at the source.

## Why It Matters

Waiting until you get sick to care about your immune system is both stressful and costly. Integrating low-cost, natural habits with clean personal hygiene lets you build a strong daily defense against common airborne viruses.
