Framework: 4US (Useful, Urgent, Unique, Ultra-specific) for lists, tips and roundups.
Structure the body for a news reader:
- Lead with the single most useful, specific item or takeaway.
- Present the list as numbered or short bold-led items, each with a concrete detail (number, name, date, place) from the source.
- Use Urgent only if the source gives a real deadline or time pressure. Use Unique only for what the source says is new or different. Do not force all four U's.
- Make the title useful and ultra-specific; keep it factual.
