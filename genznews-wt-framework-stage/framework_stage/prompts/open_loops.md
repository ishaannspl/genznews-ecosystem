Framework: OPEN_LOOPS (cliffhanger structure for long reads).
Structure the body for a news reader:
- Open with the central question the story answers, phrased from the source's facts. Do not tease anything the source does not answer.
- Split the body into 3 to 5 short sections. End each section by setting up the next fact (a question or a hint of what comes next), then pay it off at the top of the next section.
- Close the final loop with the answer or the current state of play.
Every loop must be closed using facts from the source. No clickbait, no withheld key facts.
