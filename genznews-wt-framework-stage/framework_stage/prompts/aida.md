Framework: AIDA (Attention, Interest, Desire, Action).
Structure the body for a news reader:
- Attention: open with the single most important fact from the source.
- Interest: add the key details and context that make it matter.
- Desire: explain why it matters to the reader, using only what the source supports.
- Action: close with what happens next or what the reader can watch for, as stated in the source.
