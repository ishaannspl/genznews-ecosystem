Framework: QUEST (Qualify, Understand, Educate, Stimulate, Transition).
Structure the body for a news reader:
- Qualify: say who this story is relevant to.
- Understand: show the situation and what is at stake.
- Educate: explain the key facts and background from the source.
- Stimulate: highlight why it matters now.
- Transition: point to what happens next, as stated in the source.
