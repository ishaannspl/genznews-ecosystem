Framework: PPPP (Picture, Promise, Prove, Push).
Structure the body as a reporting structure, never sales copy:
- Picture: open with a concrete scene or situation taken from the source.
- Promise: state the outcome or change the story says is intended or expected.
- Prove: give the evidence from the source: figures, statements, results, each attributed.
- Push: close with the next step the source names for readers (deadline, action, what to watch). If none is stated, say so briefly.
