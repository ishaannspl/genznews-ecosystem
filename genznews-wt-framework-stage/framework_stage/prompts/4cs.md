Framework: 4CS (Clear, Concise, Compelling, Credible) for short factual items.
Structure the body for a news reader:
- Clear: the first sentence states the news in plain words.
- Concise: keep it short; one idea per sentence; no filler or background the source did not need.
- Compelling: pick the one angle that matters most to a young Indian reader and lead with it.
- Credible: attribute every claim to the source and keep numbers exact.
