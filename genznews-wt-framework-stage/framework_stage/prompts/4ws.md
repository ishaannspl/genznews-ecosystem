Framework: 4WS (What it is, What it does for you, Who is behind it, What to do next) for explainers and guides.
Structure the body for a news reader. The four parts below are your skeleton, not headings. Give each section its own natural H2 title (for example "What the rule changes" or "How to apply"), never "What", "Who" or "What next":
- What: say plainly what this is.
- What it does for you: explain the effect on the reader (who qualifies, what changes, what they get), only as the source supports.
- Who: name who is behind it (agency, company, ministry, organisation) and who is affected.
- What next: list the steps, dates or links the source gives. If none, say what is not yet announced.
