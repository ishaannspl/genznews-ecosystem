## The Breakdown

Sahil Wakode, a B.Tech student from a Dalit community, died by suicide on September 18, 2026. His family claims he faced caste-based harassment from a professor, leading to a formal complaint under the Scheduled Castes and Scheduled Tribes Act.

The incident has triggered outrage among students and activists, criticizing IITs for their inadequate handling of caste discrimination. As a result, the implicated professor has been suspended, but calls for systemic change are growing louder.
