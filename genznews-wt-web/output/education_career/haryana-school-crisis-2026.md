## The Breakdown

In the small village of Mannaki, Haryana, children attend school in a walkway, balancing notebooks on their laps due to a lack of proper classrooms. The government school, built in 1985, suffers from dilapidated conditions, including broken blackboards and flooded kitchens, making it almost impossible for lessons to take place indoors.

This problem extends beyond Mannaki, as a significant gap in India's education funding leads to a staggering number of government schools lacking basic facilities. With vacant teaching positions and deteriorating infrastructure, many families are opting for private education, further widening the gap in educational quality across the country.
