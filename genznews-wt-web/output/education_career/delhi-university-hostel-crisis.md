## The Breakdown

A recent tragedy in New Delhi revealed the severe lack of hostel accommodations at Delhi University, where only 10 out of over 80 colleges offer housing. The existing hostels can barely cater to the thousands of students needing a place to live.

With only 7-8 postgraduate hostels available, the situation is dire. Many students are forced into overpriced and unsafe paying guest arrangements, which have become a lucrative business due to the housing shortages around the university.

## Why It Matters

For students, this is more than just a housing issue; it's a matter of safety and well-being. The lack of sufficient hostels affects mental health, academic performance, and financial stability. It's a call to action for both university administration and the government to prioritize student accommodation.
