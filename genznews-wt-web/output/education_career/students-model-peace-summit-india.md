## The Breakdown

The first Model Peace Summit at King’s College India saw 141 students from various states negotiating peace through immersive experiences. Over four days, they tackled conflict resolution in innovative ways, moving beyond traditional lectures to active participation.

Instead of simply discussing what peace is, delegates explored what it requires from them personally. Activities ranged from moot courts to restorative justice sessions, culminating in a personal commitment to peace, showcasing that young people can lead meaningful change.

## Why It Matters

This summit isn't just an event; it’s a model for youth empowerment. By giving students the tools to practice peace, it fosters a generation that understands the importance of dialogue, empathy, and responsibility, skills that can reshape communities and societies. This is about cultivating future leaders who learn by doing, not just listening.
