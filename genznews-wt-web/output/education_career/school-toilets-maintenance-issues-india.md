## The Hook

Toilets in schools? Awesome. But if they don’t work, what’s the point? This issue is more than just a plumbing problem; it impacts education.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- India has significantly improved school toilet access since 2014, but maintenance is lagging.
- Over 61,000 schools lack functional girls' toilets, affecting girls’ attendance.
- Discrepancies in data on toilet functionality highlight a need for better maintenance practices.

## The Breakdown

Since the launch of the Swachh Bharat Mission in 2014, India has made great strides in school sanitation, with 98.5% of schools having girls' toilets. However, maintenance remains a huge issue, with thousands of toilets not functioning properly.

Data reveals that 61,000 schools have non-working girls' toilets, which is a barrier for many girls, especially in rural areas. The disparity in toilet access and maintenance across states points to a larger systemic problem that needs addressing.

## Why It Matters

For young people, especially girls, reliable sanitation facilities in schools are crucial for education and well-being. Ensuring these toilets are functional can help reduce dropout rates and improve overall educational outcomes.
