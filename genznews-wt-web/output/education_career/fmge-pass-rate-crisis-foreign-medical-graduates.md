The Foreign Medical Graduate Examination (FMGE) pass rate has dropped to a shocking 12.4%, leading to widespread protests and concern among students. Many candidates reported that the exam was unexpectedly difficult, with new video modules and complex questions that didn’t align with their training.

Students like Dr. Chaynika Soni shared experiences of walking out of the exam hall overwhelmed, while others noted significant logistical issues on test day, such as inadequate facilities and harsh conditions. The lack of transparency around the exam's structure and results has left many feeling frustrated and helpless.

## Why It Matters

For students considering studying abroad for medical degrees, the FMGE's rising difficulty signals a need for better preparation and support. This crisis could redefine the landscape of medical education and practice in India, highlighting the importance of adaptability in academic environments.
