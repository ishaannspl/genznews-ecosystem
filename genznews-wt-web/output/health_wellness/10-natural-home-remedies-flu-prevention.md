## The Breakdown

Staying healthy during an outbreak comes down to preparation rather than panic. Simple kitchen remedies such as raw garlic in the morning, fresh basil leaves, and warm turmeric milk at night offer practical ways to strengthen immune resistance over time.

Traditional herbs like Giloi can be boiled into a warm drink with black pepper and rock salt to help fight off seasonal infections. Paired with daily Vitamin C from citrus fruits or Amla juice, these habits give your body a steady nutritional boost.

Physical routines play an equally critical role. Practicing deep breathing exercises like Pranayam and maintaining clean hands with soap or sanitizers after touching shared surfaces cuts down exposure risks drastically.

## Why It Matters

Wellness is all about proactive prevention rather than scrambling for emergency fixes. Integrating basic herbs, functional movement, and solid personal hygiene into your routine builds long-term defense against airborne bugs.
