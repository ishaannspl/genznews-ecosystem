## The Breakdown

Leprosy cases in India have seen a decline, with the incidence rate dropping significantly since 2005. However, the disease is still being transmitted in various regions, hindering the country's health goals for 2023-2027.

Efforts like door-to-door screenings have identified thousands of cases, but stigma and misdiagnosis remain barriers to care. The government is using digital surveillance and considering new vaccines to help tackle this persistent issue.

## Why It Matters

For young people, understanding the ongoing challenges of leprosy in India highlights the importance of health awareness and the impact of stigma on disease management.
