## The Breakdown

The 2026 Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine has been awarded to Karl Deisseroth, Peter Hegemann, and Georg Nagel for their pioneering work in optogenetics. This innovative technique enables scientists to control nerve cells using light, marking a significant leap in neuroscience.

Their discoveries have broad implications, allowing for targeted studies of conditions like depression, schizophrenia, and neurodegenerative diseases. The Nobel Assembly recognized how their findings help researchers better understand neural circuits related to behaviors and emotions.
