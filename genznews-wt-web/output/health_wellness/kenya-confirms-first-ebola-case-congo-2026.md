## The Hook

Ebola has officially crossed into Kenya for the first time in this outbreak, and health officials are racing to trace dozens of potential contacts.

## TL;DR (Quick Hits)

- Kenya confirmed its first imported Ebola case after a citizen traveled from Congo via Uganda and passed away in Nairobi.
- Health authorities are actively monitoring 28 direct contacts and tracking 23 airline passengers for the standard 21-day quarantine period.
- The World Health Organization warns this is the fastest-growing Ebola outbreak in history, driven by the rare Bundibugyo virus strain.

## The Breakdown

Kenya's Health Ministry confirmed that a citizen arriving from Congo via Uganda fell ill and tested positive for the Ebola virus at a Nairobi hospital. The patient tragically passed away, prompting officials to immediately place the country on high alert for additional cases.

Health workers have identified 28 close contacts, including family members and medical staff, alongside 23 airline passengers currently being tracked. All identified contacts are placed in isolation for a 21-day monitoring period to contain further transmission.
