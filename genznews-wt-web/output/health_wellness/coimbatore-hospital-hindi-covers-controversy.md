## The Breakdown

The Coimbatore Medical College Hospital recently swapped out its brown paper medicine covers after Hindi instructions prompted criticism. DMK leader T.R.B. Raja called out the inclusion of Hindi, claiming it contradicts the state's language policy.

Hospital officials noted that the covers had been in use without issue until now. They stated the pharmacy was instructed to revert to new covers featuring only Tamil and English instructions, reflecting the ongoing tensions around language use in Tamil Nadu.

## Why It Matters
