## The Breakdown

Rezolv Energy, backed by Actis, has signed off on a hefty financing package of €561 million (around $637 million) for its Dama Solar project in western Romania. This 1.3 GW solar installation is gearing up for construction, with operations set to start in the latter half of 2028.

Once up and running, the Dama Solar project is projected to produce approximately 1,800 GWh of electricity each year, significantly boosting renewable energy output in the region. This financing is part of a larger trend of massive investments flowing into clean energy initiatives across Europe.
