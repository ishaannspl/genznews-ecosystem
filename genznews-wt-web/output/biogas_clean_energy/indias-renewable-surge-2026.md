## The Breakdown

In the first half of 2026, India made headlines by adding 50.6 GW of solar module capacity and 9.7 GW of solar cell capacity. This surge highlights the dominance of top manufacturers, who control nearly 60% of the market.

The battery storage scene also exploded, with 8.2 GWh added, which is almost 84 times what was installed during the same period last year. With initiatives like the Green Energy Corridor Phase III, the country is gearing up for a renewable energy revolution, allowing for the evacuation of up to 135 GW of renewable energy.
