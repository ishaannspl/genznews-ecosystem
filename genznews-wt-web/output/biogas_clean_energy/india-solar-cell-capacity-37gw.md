## The Breakdown

The Ministry of New and Renewable Energy (MNRE) has just updated its Approved List of Models and Manufacturers (ALMM), boosting solar cell capacity to 37,394 MW. This expansion includes an additional 529 MW of Mono PERC solar cells, reflecting a strong commitment to renewable energy.

This is the 11th revision of the ALMM, showcasing India's dedication to enhancing its clean energy infrastructure. With these updates, the country is solidifying its position as a leader in solar technology and sustainable energy solutions.

## Why It Matters

For young people, this expansion means more job opportunities in the growing clean energy sector. It's also a step toward a more sustainable future, reducing reliance on fossil fuels and combating climate change.
