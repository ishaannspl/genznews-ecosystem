## The Breakdown

Delhi's air quality index (AQI) reached 187, slipping into the high end of the moderate category, with some areas hitting much worse levels. Monitoring stations reported a peak AQI of 273 in Mundka, while neighboring Noida recorded an even higher score of 281.

Experts attribute this decline to low wind speeds and the onset of winter, which is known for trapping pollutants. Predictions suggest that Delhi's AQI will degrade further, likely hitting the poor category in the coming days as farm fires and other emissions ramp up.

## Why It Matters

For young people, especially those in urban areas, this worsening air quality isn't just an environmental issue; it's a health risk. Increasing pollution levels can lead to respiratory problems and long-term health issues. Staying informed helps you make better choices about outdoor activities and overall wellness.
