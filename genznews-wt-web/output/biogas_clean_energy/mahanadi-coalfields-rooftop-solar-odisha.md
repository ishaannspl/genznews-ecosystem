## The Breakdown

Mahanadi Coalfields (MCL), part of Coal India, is ramping up its green game by inviting bids for grid-connected rooftop solar setups. These projects will be installed across 15 non-residential buildings at the Jagannath Open Cast Project located in Odisha.

Bidders have until October 21, 2026, to submit their proposals, marking a significant step towards diversifying energy sources in the region and promoting sustainability in coal operations.

## Why It Matters

This initiative isn't just about solar energy; it's a step towards reducing carbon footprints and enhancing energy efficiency in Odisha. For young people, it signals a growing commitment to renewable energy, which could open up future job opportunities in clean tech and sustainability fields.
